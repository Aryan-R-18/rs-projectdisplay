About the project

The Remotely Operated Underwater Vehicle (ROUV) is an advanced submersible robotic system designed for real-time underwater exploration, surveillance and structural assessment.

Its primary purpose is to inspect the Hirakud Dam and detect structural anomalies such as cracks, removing the risks of human divers working in high-pressure, turbulent environments.

The ROUV offers remote and autonomous operation, HD video transmission, SONAR-based mapping and stable navigation in dynamic underwater conditions.

Approach

v2.0 builds on the field learnings of v1.0.

v1.0 · Initial prototype

ROUV v1.0

  • 6-metre underwater depth capability
  • 1080p real-time video surveillance
  • Position-hold system stable up to 12 m/s currents
  • Embedded environmental sensors for temperature variation monitoring
  • Six-axis manoeuvrability for precise control
v2.0 · Enhanced version

ROUV v2.0

  • Higher depth capability for high-pressure environments
  • Waypoint-based navigation for autonomous missions
  • Improved depth sensing and object detection algorithms
  • Position hold stable up to 30 m/s currents
  • Neutral buoyancy for efficient energy use
  • Automated mission planning with live HD video streaming
  • Integrated leak detection system

Design

A modular aluminium extrusion chassis offering strength, low weight and expandability.

Structural frame

  • Built from 20×20 aluminium extrusion
  • H-plates, corner brackets and T-nuts for rigidity
  • Four external buoyant tubes for stability
  • Central sealed main tube houses the electronics

Propulsion & motion

  • 8× FLYCCI FA3115 400KV BLDC motors
  • 2× FLYYWOO GOKU 45A ESCs
  • Holonomic motion system for multi-directional underwater movement, rare in Indian ROUV designs

Electronics

  • Raspberry Pi 5 (8GB) for computation, mapping and image processing
  • Arduino UNO R4 for low-level motor and sensor control
  • HD camera encased in a glass globe
  • Long-duration LiPo battery system

Applications

Infrastructure inspection

Underwater assessment of dams, bridges and pipelines.

Defence & security

Surveillance of underwater zones to detect threats or objects.

Environmental research

Monitoring aquatic ecosystems and pollution levels.

Oil & gas industry

Inspection of offshore rigs and underwater pipelines.
